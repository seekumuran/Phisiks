# PHISIKS release log

## 1.1.0 (prepared 2026-11-27; not yet tagged or published)

**Product direction.** PHISIKS is now presented as a **general-purpose 3D engine** with an editor. The main application, `Phisiks`, opens an empty document with development tools and no demo content. The sample scenes, the four showcases and the two engineering projects (Project Marina, Old-McDonald) are separate programs built on the engine.

### Removed demo presentation

- The "PHISIKS LAB" branding: menu-bar title plate, window title, CPack summary, user-facing strings, report header.
- The Lab landing experience: it booted into the first-person Sandbox with the lesson playlist ("CLASSROOM PLAYLIST"), the SHOWCASE playlist and lesson captions.
- The Lab's *Simulation > Lessons / Showcases / Projects* menus and the `CAD` menu.
- Showcase scenes hosted inside the main application. They remain standalone executables.
- References to Project Marina and Old-McDonald in the main application.
- In both projects:
  - every in-scene **text callout** (40 removed, with their data and the callout renderer);
  - the presentation-mode **title blocks** and **legends**;
  - the LCD subtitles and explanatory paragraphs in their panels;
  - "Presentation" camera presets, renamed "Perspective".

The helipad and dock floor markings are 3D geometry and stay. Operating controls and telemetry stay: cameras, mission transport, stage list with what drives each stage, counters, display toggles, subsystems, scene-file buttons.

### Preserved functionality

All engine systems are unchanged apart from the fixes listed below. Every sample scene, showcase, project scene and CAD workspace still exists and is covered by the runtime tests: the mission (A* + IK), explode views, visibility, picking, save / reopen. The Lab studio's tools are all in the editor: hierarchy, inspector for every component, gizmo, physics / render settings, telemetry, replay timeline and 4-way view.

### Editor (new in 1.1)

- **Layout:** menu bar and tool bar; docked SCENE (hierarchy), SETTINGS or workspace panel, INSPECTOR and OUTPUT (console / telemetry / timeline); resizable with splitters. The main camera is fitted to the centre viewport. DPI-aware scaling (`PHISIKS_UI_SCALE`).
- **File:** New, Open, Save, Save as (in-app file browser), Reopen, Quit. An unsaved-changes prompt appears on quit, window close, New, Open and Reload. Saving goes through the workspace hooks.
- **Edit and Create:** delete with confirmation, deselect, frame selection (F); create empty / cube / sphere / plane / rigid cube / directional, point and spot lights.
- **Hierarchy and inspector:**
  - hierarchy search, drag-to-parent and a context menu;
  - inspector: add component, a mesh picker over the asset folders, the material's opacity;
  - rotation fields that pass ±90°.
- **Tool bar and output:** play / pause / step-frame / reload; 4-way view framed to the scene; console with level and text filters.
- **Library API:**
  - `EditorApp.h`: shared command line, `Host`, `Run`, `--open`, `--assets`;
  - `Editor::AppConfig` (menus, side panel, inset viewport);
  - `Editor::WorkspaceExtension` with persistence hooks.

### Other fixes made during 1.1

- **Third-party licences:**
  - `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` listed only the licences' names, and the archives carried no licence texts, although MIT, BSD-3 (PhysX) and zlib (GLFW) require the notice with binary copies;
  - `fetch_deps.py` now collects every licence at its pinned version;
  - packages ship `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/`.
- The engine's **R** (reload) and **N** (next scene) hotkeys discarded edits without asking. They are off in editor-hosted programs (`AppOptions::sceneHotkeys`).
- `fetch_deps.py` failed on "externally managed" Pythons (PEP 668: Homebrew on macOS, Ubuntu 24.04). glad2 now falls back to a private virtual environment. This was the cause of the first macOS CI failure.
- Editor issues found from screenshots of the real executables:
  - minimising the window collapsed the scene panel;
  - DPI-scaled columns squeezed the viewport to 30 %;
  - the quality combo showed "Medium" while the renderer ran the High preset;
  - the first-person crosshair was drawn in the orbit camera;
  - the ground grid had moiré from 20 lines per metre;
  - buttons and slider values were clipped at 150 % scaling.
- CI:
  - a macOS job;
  - runtime-test jobs on Mesa llvmpipe (Linux) and macOS;
  - the results published as annotations (summary, renderer, failed checks, gdb backtrace) instead of being hidden behind `continue-on-error`.

### Outstanding limitations

See [KNOWN_ISSUES.md](KNOWN_ISSUES.md). Most important:

- macOS rendering is unverified;
- no undo / redo;
- no asset-import pipeline;
- play does not restore the scene on stop;
- scene files do not store joints, scripts, navigation, terrain or animation components;
- unsigned binaries.

3. Optional: attach the locally built `-win64-physx` archive, built with a released Visual Studio (H7).
4. Review the draft release and publish it.
