# Third-party notices

PHISIKS itself is MIT-licensed (`LICENSE`). It builds against the following third-party libraries. They are placed in `vendor/` (not committed: `python tools/fetch_deps.py` downloads the portable ones at pinned versions; the Windows PhysX and GLFW binaries are placed by hand, see `BUILD_WINDOWS.md`). They are used unmodified, apart from the ImGuizmo compatibility patch below, and remain under their own licences.

**Licence texts.** `tools/fetch_deps.py` collects each library's licence text, at the pinned version, into `vendor/licenses/`. Release packages ship them in `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/`. If you redistribute binaries built another way, include those texts as well.

The assets in `assets/` and `projects/assets/` are generated by `tools/assetgen` and `projects/tools` (original, CC0).
