They are placed in `vendor/` (not committed; see `docs/BUILD.md`), used unmodified, and remain under their own licences. If you redistribute binaries, include the licence texts from each project's upstream distribution.

| Library | Used for | Licence | Upstream |
|---|---|---|---|

| GLFW 3 | window, OpenGL context, input events (`vendor/GLFW`) | zlib/libpng | https://www.glfw.org |
